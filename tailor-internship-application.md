# Tailor an internship application to one posting

## When to use
- Jayden pastes a job description or link and says he is applying.
- He needs CV bullets reordered or reworded for a specific firm and role.
- He needs answers for an application portal ("Why us?", "Tell us about a time...") with word or character limits.
- He has a video interview stage coming and wants the written story to match what he will say.
- Do not use for general CV advice with no posting attached, or for interview practice on its own (that is a separate skill).

## Inputs needed
- **The posting** (required). Paste the full job description. If missing, ask: "Paste the full job description, not just the title. The requirements section matters most."
- **What the portal asks for** (required). CV only, cover letter, written questions, or all of them, plus any word or character limits. If missing, ask: "What does the form actually ask for, and are there limits?"
- **Deadline** (optional). If missing, ask once. If he doesn't know, flag it as a gap in the output.
- **Anything new since the last application** (optional). New project milestone, new grade, a conversation with someone at the firm. Do not ask; use it if offered.

## Evidence bank (Jayden's stories, use these instead of asking him again)
- **AIA, Investment Accounting intern (2021).** Position and cash reconciliation across Thailand, Malaysia, Australia and New Zealand, plus CCS checking. Proves accuracy and control.
- **NEXEA, Venture Capital intern (2022).** Due diligence and fundraising support on seed to Series A deals in tech, luxury retail and trade. Proves judgment with incomplete information.
- **GUIDE, Research Analyst intern (2023).** Researched the Southeast Asia startup ecosystem. Proves structured research. Always name the employer GUIDE.
- **Liaison Lead, student business incubator.** Moderated forums, ran records and databases, co-organised workshops. Proves organisation and stakeholder work.
- **365-day Excel Reels series.** Proves consistency and the ability to explain one idea simply.
- **Forget (AI calendar app) and The Ledger Lands (Excel learning game).** Proves initiative and that he builds tools he actually uses.
- **Hospitality, Alpine Resort Victoria and Dundees Restaurant (2025 to 2026).** Proves composure with customers and fast-paced teamwork.
- **MSc Business Analytics for Decision Makers, NCI (from Sept 2026).** Finance degree plus analytics.
- **Availability.** Internships and graduate programmes now; full-time from January 2028.

## Steps
1. Read the posting and pull out the top five things the firm is screening for, using their own words.
2. Match each one to the strongest story in the evidence bank. If a requirement has no matching story, mark it as a gap rather than stretching one.
3. Pick the three or four CV bullets that matter most for this role and rewrite them so the result leads, then the proof, then the detail.
4. Write each requested answer in Jayden's voice. Short sentences, plain words, full stops and commas, no em dashes, no colons in prose, nothing corporate. Respect every word and character limit.
5. Write one "why this firm" line that names something specific to the firm or team, not generic praise. If you cannot find something specific in the posting, say so and ask him for one fact.
6. Check every line against the posting once more and cut anything that would fit any company.
7. Close with one line saying which answer is strongest and which one he should read aloud before submitting.

## Output contract
- Length: one screen per output type. CV bullets and answers only, no essay about the process.
- Must include: the match table, the rewritten bullets, each requested answer with its word count, gaps flagged, deadline if known.
- Must not include: invented numbers, invented achievements, generic phrases ("passionate about", "fast-paced environment", "team player"), em dashes.
- Skeleton:

| What they want | Best story | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | Strong / OK / Gap |

**CV bullets for [Firm]**
- ...

**[Question text]** (limit: X words, used: Y)
...

**Gaps**: ...
**Strongest line**: ...

## One worked example
Input: Citi Services Summer Analyst, Dublin. Posting stresses attention to detail, client service, Excel, interest in operations. Portal asks "Why Citi Services?" in 150 words.
Output:
| Attention to detail | AIA reconciliations across four markets | Strong |
| Client service | Front of house at Dundees, visitor experience at Alpine Resort | Strong |
| Excel | 365-day Excel Reels series | Strong |
| Interest in operations | AIA CCS checking | OK |
CV bullet, AIA: "Reconciled positions and cash across four Asia-Pacific markets and checked Centralized Clearing System records for accuracy."
Gaps: no direct client-facing finance role. Lead with AIA, use hospitality as the client proof.
Strongest line: the AIA bullet. Read the 150-word answer aloud once before submitting.
